Accepted Insurance Providers

SAREC Medicare Centre works with the following insurance companies to serve our patients:

🛡️

Jubilee Insurance Company

We accept Jubilee Health Insurance for outpatient, in-patient, and specialist consultations. Present your insurance card at reception for direct billing.

🛡️

SANLAM

Patients covered by SANLAM health insurance are welcome for a full range of medical services. Claims are processed directly on your behalf.

🛡️

Bank of Uganda

Bank of Uganda medical scheme members can access our outpatient, specialist, and in-patient services.

🛡️

Liberty Blue

We accept Liberty Blue health cover for consultations, diagnostics, and treatment across all our departments.

🛡️

Prudential Life Assurance

Prudential Life Assurance policyholders can receive covered medical services at our centre with direct claim processing.

🛡️

GA Insurance

GA Insurance health plan holders are accepted for outpatient, in-patient, and specialist care at SAREC Medicare Centre.

If your insurer is not listed above, please contact us at 0700-665522 to confirm whether your insurance is accepted.

Payment Schemes

SAREC Medicare Centre offers three convenient payment options for all patients:

💵

1. Cash Payment

Walk-in patients can pay for services in cash at the time of their visit. Our fees are transparent and affordable — no hidden charges.

🛡️

2. Insurance

Patients covered by any of our accepted insurance providers (listed above) can access services under their health plan with direct billing at reception.

💳

3. Pre-payment Scheme

Make a deposit of minimum UGX 500,000 and your account is debited for each service until the balance is used up. Enjoy a UGX 5,000 discount on every consultation visit under this scheme.

Antenatal Care Package

SAREC Medicare Centre offers a comprehensive Antenatal Package for expectant mothers — covering all routine antenatal visits, laboratory investigations, ultrasound scans, and delivery services. Contact us for full details and pricing.
