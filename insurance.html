Payment Schemes

SAREC Medicare Centre offers three convenient payment options for all patients:

1. Cash Payment

Walk-in patients can pay for services in cash at the time of their visit. Our fees are transparent and affordable — no hidden charges.

️

2. Insurance

Patients covered by any of our accepted insurance providers (listed above) can access services under their health plan with direct billing at reception.

3. Pre-payment Scheme

Make a deposit of minimum UGX 500,000 and your account is debited for each service until the balance is used up. Enjoy a UGX 5,000 discount on every consultation visit under this scheme.
