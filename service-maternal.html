Maternal & Child Health

Compassionate, expert care for mothers and children — from pregnancy and safe delivery through immunisations, growth monitoring and paediatric care.

Care for Mother and Child at Every Stage

Pregnancy, childbirth and early childhood are some of the most important moments in a family’s life. SAREC Medicare Centre provides a complete maternal and child health programme led by qualified obstetricians, paediatricians, midwives and nurses — supporting mothers and babies with skilled clinical care in a warm and welcoming environment.

Antenatal Care (ANC)

Regular antenatal visits help us detect and manage any concerns early, so that you can have a healthy pregnancy and a safe delivery.

  • Booking visit with full history, examination and antenatal profile (blood group, haemoglobin, HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B, urinalysis)
  • Routine follow-up visits with weight, blood pressure, foetal heart monitoring and growth assessment
  • Obstetric ultrasound for dating, anomaly screening and growth monitoring
  • Tetanus immunisation, iron and folic acid supplementation, and malaria prevention in pregnancy
  • PMTCT — prevention of mother-to-child transmission of HIV
  • Health education on nutrition, danger signs in pregnancy, breastfeeding and birth preparedness

Labour & Delivery

Our maternity unit is staffed around the clock by experienced midwives and obstetricians.

  • Skilled birth attendance for normal (vaginal) deliveries
  • Operative deliveries including Caesarean section, when medically indicated
  • Continuous foetal monitoring and partograph-guided labour management
  • Management of obstetric emergencies and stabilisation for referral where required
  • Pain relief and supportive companionship during labour

Postnatal & Newborn Care

  • Postnatal reviews for mother and baby
  • Newborn examination, BCG and polio immunisation at birth
  • Breastfeeding support and counselling
  • Care of the umbilical cord, jaundice screening and weight monitoring
  • Family planning counselling and provision

Child Health & Immunisation

Children grow and change quickly — our paediatric team supports that journey at every step.

  • Routine immunisations according to the Uganda National Expanded Programme on Immunisation (UNEPI)
  • Growth and developmental monitoring with regular weight, height and milestone assessments
  • Treatment of common childhood illnesses — malaria, pneumonia, diarrhoea, skin and ear infections
  • Nutritional assessment and counselling — including management of malnutrition
  • Neonatal and paediatric in-patient care when admission is required

Family Planning & Women’s Wellness

  • Counselling and provision of modern family planning methods (oral, injectable, implants, IUCDs)
  • Cervical cancer screening (VIA / Pap smear) and HPV vaccination
  • Pre-conception care and fertility counselling
  • Adolescent health and reproductive health education
