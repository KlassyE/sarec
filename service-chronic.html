Long-Term Care for Chronic Health Conditions

Chronic diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, asthma, and heart disease require ongoing attention, regular monitoring, and a strong partnership between patient and doctor. At SAREC Medicare Centre, we provide structured, compassionate chronic disease management programmes to help you live well and stay in control of your health.

Conditions We Manage

  • Diabetes (Type 1 & Type 2) — Blood sugar monitoring, HbA1c testing, medication management, dietary guidance, and complication screening
  • Hypertension (High Blood Pressure) — Regular BP monitoring, medication adjustment, lifestyle modification counseling, and cardiovascular risk assessment
  • Asthma & Chronic Respiratory Conditions — Inhaler technique training, action plans, trigger avoidance education, and regular review
  • Heart Disease — Monitoring, medication management, lifestyle counseling, and timely referral for specialist care
  • Chronic Kidney Disease — Kidney function monitoring, medication review, and dietary advice
  • Arthritis & Chronic Pain — Pain management, medication, and referral to physiotherapy when needed

Our Approach

Managing a chronic condition is a journey — not a single visit. Our approach includes:

  • Regular check-up schedule — We create a personalised follow-up plan so you never miss a critical test or review
  • Medication review — We assess whether your current medications are working effectively and make adjustments as needed
  • Laboratory monitoring — Ongoing tests (blood sugar, kidney function, cholesterol, etc.) to track your condition
  • Lifestyle counseling — Practical guidance on diet, exercise, stress management, and healthy habits
  • Patient education — Helping you understand your condition so you can manage it confidently
  • Specialist referrals — When your condition requires specialised care, we facilitate timely referrals to trusted specialists

Why Regular Monitoring Matters

Many chronic conditions — especially hypertension and diabetes — are "silent" diseases. They may not cause obvious symptoms until serious damage has occurred. Regular check-ups and tests at SAREC Medicare Centre can catch problems early, prevent complications, and keep you on track for better health outcomes.

Affordable, Consistent Care

We understand that managing a chronic condition can be financially challenging. We keep our consultation and monitoring fees affordable so that cost is never a barrier to the ongoing care you need. Ask about our chronic care packages for regular patients.
