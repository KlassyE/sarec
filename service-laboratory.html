Laboratory Services

Accurate diagnostics performed on-site by qualified laboratory technologists — supporting timely clinical decisions and better patient outcomes.

Reliable Diagnostics, Delivered Quickly

The laboratory is at the heart of accurate diagnosis and effective treatment. At SAREC Medicare Centre, our in-house laboratory is equipped with modern analysers and staffed by qualified technologists who follow strict quality-control procedures. Whether you have been referred by one of our doctors or are visiting for routine screening, you can expect prompt, reliable results.

Haematology

Tests that assess the cellular components of blood — essential for diagnosing infections, anaemia, and a wide range of conditions.

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) — red cells, white cells, platelets and indices
  • Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR) — marker of inflammation
  • Blood grouping and Rhesus typing
  • Peripheral blood film — microscopic review of blood cells
  • Sickling test and haemoglobin electrophoresis

Clinical Chemistry & Biochemistry

Blood and urine chemistry tests that evaluate organ function and metabolic health.

  • Liver function tests (LFTs) — ALT, AST, bilirubin, ALP, albumin
  • Kidney (renal) function tests — urea, creatinine, electrolytes
  • Random & fasting blood sugar, HbA1c — diabetes screening and monitoring
  • Lipid profile — cholesterol and triglycerides for cardiovascular risk
  • Uric acid, calcium, magnesium, phosphate

Microbiology & Parasitology

Identification of infectious causes of illness to guide accurate treatment.

  • Malaria rapid diagnostic test (RDT) and microscopy for malaria parasites
  • Urinalysis & urine microscopy — urinary tract infections, kidney disease screening
  • Stool analysis — intestinal parasites, occult blood, H. pylori antigen
  • Widal test — typhoid screening
  • Brucella, H. pylori and other rapid serological tests
  • Wound swabs and culture referrals where indicated

Serology & Rapid Tests

  • HIV testing — confidential rapid tests with pre- and post-test counselling
  • Hepatitis B & C screening
  • Syphilis (RPR/VDRL) screening
  • Pregnancy tests (urine & serum β-hCG)
  • COVID-19 antigen testing (subject to availability)

Antenatal & Pre-Operative Profiles

Bundled investigations to support safer pregnancies and surgical procedures — including blood group, haemoglobin level, HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B and urinalysis.

Quality You Can Trust

All laboratory work at SAREC Medicare Centre follows standard operating procedures with internal quality controls. Samples are handled with strict attention to integrity and patient confidentiality, and most routine results are available the same day. Where specialised tests are required, samples are sent to accredited reference laboratories and results returned to your treating clinician promptly.
