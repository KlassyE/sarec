Laboratory Services

Accurate, on-site diagnostic testing to support your doctor with reliable results — from routine screens to specialised assays.

Modern On-Site Diagnostic Laboratory

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. At SAREC Medicare Centre, our on-site laboratory is equipped to perform a wide range of diagnostic tests, giving our doctors the information they need to treat you correctly — and giving you results fast.

Tests We Offer

Our laboratory provides the following diagnostic services:

  • Malaria Testing — Rapid Diagnostic Tests (RDTs) and blood film microscopy
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) — Full haemogram to assess overall health
  • Blood Glucose — Fasting and random blood sugar, HbA1c for diabetes monitoring
  • Liver Function Tests (LFTs) — ALT, AST, bilirubin, and more
  • Kidney Function Tests (KFTs) — Creatinine, BUN, and electrolytes
  • Urinalysis — Comprehensive urine analysis for infections, kidney health, and pregnancy
  • Pregnancy Tests — Rapid urine-based pregnancy detection
  • HIV Testing — Confidential rapid HIV screening and confirmatory tests
  • Lipid Profile — Cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL, and LDL levels
  • Widal Test — Typhoid fever screening
  • Stool Analysis — For parasites, ova, and other infections
  • Blood Grouping — ABO and Rhesus factor determination

Fast, Same-Day Results

Most routine tests are processed on-site and results are available within hours — often while you wait. For specialised tests that require referral to a partner laboratory, we ensure timely collection and delivery of results.

Quality You Can Trust

Our laboratory is operated by trained, qualified technicians who follow strict quality-control procedures. We use reliable, well-maintained equipment and adhere to standard operating protocols to ensure accurate, reproducible results every time.

No Doctor Visit Required for Lab Tests

While most lab tests are ordered by our doctors during a consultation, you may also walk in for specific tests (such as malaria, blood sugar, or HIV testing) without a prior consultation. Ask at reception for details.
