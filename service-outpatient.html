General Outpatient Consultations

Home›Services›General Outpatient Consultations

Walk-In Consultations for All Ages

At SAREC Medicare Centre, our general outpatient consultation service is the cornerstone of everything we do. Whether you're feeling unwell, need a routine check-up, or have concerns about a recurring health issue, our experienced doctors are here to help — no appointment necessary.

What We Treat

Our doctors are trained to diagnose and manage a wide range of conditions, including:

  • Malaria, typhoid, and other common infections
  • Upper respiratory tract infections (coughs, colds, flu, sore throat)
  • Urinary tract infections (UTIs)
  • Gastrointestinal issues (diarrhoea, stomach pain, acid reflux)
  • Skin conditions and allergies
  • Musculoskeletal pain and injuries
  • Headaches and migraines
  • Chronic conditions such as diabetes and hypertension (ongoing management)
  • Minor wounds, burns, and injuries

What to Expect During Your Visit

When you walk into SAREC Medicare Centre, you'll be welcomed by our reception team and registered quickly. You'll then see one of our doctors, who will:

  • Take a thorough medical history
  • Perform a physical examination
  • Order any necessary laboratory tests (available on-site)
  • Provide a clear diagnosis and explain your treatment plan
  • Write prescriptions, which can be filled at our on-site pharmacy
  • Refer you to a specialist if your condition requires it

For All Ages

We see patients of all ages — from infants and children to adults and the elderly. Whether it's your child's first fever or a grandparent's routine check-up, our team provides attentive, personalised care for every patient.

Walk-Ins Welcome

No prior appointment is needed for general consultations. Simply walk in at any time — we are open 24/7, Monday to Sunday. For your convenience, you can also book an appointment online to reduce waiting time.
