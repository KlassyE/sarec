Important Information

  • Confirmation: Our receptionist will call you within 2 hours (during working hours) to confirm your appointment.
  • Walk-ins Welcome: You do not need an appointment. Walk-ins are always welcome during our opening hours.
  • What to Bring: Your national ID or passport, any previous medical records, insurance card (if applicable), and a list of current medications.
  • Insurance Patients: Please bring your valid insurance card and pre-authorisation letter if required by your insurer.
  • Opening Hours: Open 24/7, Monday – Sunday.
  • Emergency? For urgent medical needs, skip the form and call us directly at 0700-665522.
