About SAREC Medicare Centre

A specialised medical centre serving Kyaliwajjala and the wider Kampala community since 2011 — combining experienced specialists, modern facilities, and patient-centred care.

Background

SAREC MEDICARE CONSULTANTS was established in 2011 to provide specialised medical services in Uganda and beyond. It was established in response to a growing need for high quality and comprehensive specialised medical services.

It has since been upgraded to provide a full suite of specialised services provided by medical specialists namely Three obstetricians and gynaecologists, two Paediatricians, a Physician, surgeon, general practitioners, Dental services and Radiologist. These all provide specialised clinical services in their respective specialised fields.

SAREC MEDICARE CONSULTANTS/CENTRE has both in-patient and out-patient Departments. We have good working relationship with other health care providers within and without the city where we refer patients for other specialised services not available at the centre. We also get referrals from various health care centres.

Vision, Mission & Goal

Our Vision

Healthy, productive and fulfilled life for everyone in Africa.

Our Mission

To provide high quality, affordable, safe, and highly ethical and user friendly health services to all people in need.

Our Goal

Increase access to care and treatment services to all clients in need.

Our Objectives

  • To provide medical consultancy services
  • To provide safe and affordable pharmaceutical commodities to clients
  • To offer care and treatment services to clients
  • To offer disease prevention services to clients and communities
  • To provide capacity building (training) services to health workers or pre-service trainees
