Specialised Medical Services in Uganda Since 2011

SAREC MEDICARE CONSULTANTS was established in 2011 to provide specialised medical services in Uganda and beyond. It was established in response to a growing need for high quality and comprehensive specialised medical services.

We now provide a full suite of specialised services by medical specialists — including three obstetricians and gynaecologists, two paediatricians, a physician, surgeon, general practitioners, dental services and a radiologist. SAREC MEDICARE CENTRE has both in-patient and out-patient departments.

Noteworthy, is the fact that we are the only known private health care providers that offer a complete package for cervical cancer and other prevention. This includes HPV vaccination, screening for cervical cancer, colposcopy and biopsy for histological studies and treatment of pre-cancer lesions.

MATERNITY

Convenient Antenatal, Delivery and Post Natal Services

Our maternity services monitor your pregnancy and the unborn baby's health through to the birth of your baby and for some months thereafter in the Post Natal Clinic. Every baby's birth is unique — our team of obstetricians, midwives and paediatricians support you to meet your personal birthing and care needs to ensure the best possible birthing experience.

With three obstetricians and gynaecologists and two paediatricians on our specialist team, mothers and babies receive comprehensive care at every stage — from antenatal visits and safe delivery, through postnatal care and child immunisations.
