Comprehensive Healthcare Services

SAREC Medicare Centre provides clinical and investigative services. Services range from outpatient care, obstetric and gynaecological services, paediatric, internal medicine, medical imaging, laboratory, day and inpatient care services for all patients.

General Outpatient Consultations

Walk-in consultations for patients of all ages. Our experienced doctors diagnose and treat a wide range of common illnesses, chronic conditions, infections, and injuries. We provide thorough examinations, accurate diagnoses, medical advice, and prescriptions tailored to your needs.

Read More →

Laboratory Services

Our modern on-site laboratory offers a wide range of diagnostic tests to support accurate diagnoses. Services include complete blood counts (CBC), malaria rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs), blood glucose and HbA1c, liver and kidney function tests, urinalysis, pregnancy tests, HIV testing, lipid profiles, and more. Most results are available the same day.

Read More →

Pharmacy

Our fully stocked pharmacy is conveniently located within the clinic, ensuring that you can fill your prescriptions immediately after your consultation. We carry a wide range of quality, affordable medications including antibiotics, antimalarials, painkillers, chronic disease medications, and over-the-counter products.

Read More →

Maternal & Child Health

We provide comprehensive maternal and child health services including antenatal care (ANC) visits, postnatal check-ups, childhood immunizations and vaccinations following Uganda's national schedule, growth monitoring, family planning counseling, and reproductive health services. Supporting mothers and children from pregnancy through early childhood.

Read More →

HIV/AIDS Care & Testing

SAREC Medicare Centre provides confidential HIV counseling and testing (HCT) services in a safe, supportive, and stigma-free environment. For those who test positive, we facilitate linkage to antiretroviral therapy (ART) care and provide ongoing support. We also offer prevention education and awareness programs for the community.

Read More →

Chronic Disease Management

Living with a chronic condition requires ongoing care and monitoring. We provide long-term management programs for diabetes, hypertension (high blood pressure), asthma, heart disease, and other chronic conditions. Our approach includes regular check-ups, medication review and adjustment, lifestyle counseling, and referrals when specialist care is needed.

Read More →

Obstetric & Gynaecological Services

Comprehensive obstetric and gynaecological care provided by three specialist obstetricians and gynaecologists. We provide Comprehensive Emergency Obstetric Newborn Care (CEmONC) services including antenatal care, safe deliveries, and postnatal care. We also carry out major and minor operations in our surgical theatre.

Paediatric Services

Our two paediatricians provide specialised care for infants, children and adolescents. Services include growth monitoring, immunizations, treatment of childhood illnesses, nutritional counseling, and management of chronic childhood conditions.

Surgical Services

We carry out major and minor operations in our well-equipped surgical theatre. Our surgeon provides general surgical consultations, scheduled surgeries, and emergency surgical care as needed.

Dental Services

Our dental unit offers a range of oral health services including dental check-ups, cleanings, fillings, extractions, and oral health education for patients of all ages.

Medical Imaging / Radiology

Our radiologist provides diagnostic imaging services to support accurate diagnosis. Imaging services include ultrasound scans and other radiological investigations as required for patient care.

Cervical Cancer Prevention

We are the only known private health care provider offering a complete cervical cancer prevention package. This includes HPV vaccination, screening for cervical cancer as well as other gynaecological cancers, colposcopy and biopsy for histological studies (diagnosis), and treatment of pre-cancer lesions.

Don't see what you need? Call us — we may be able to help or refer you to the right specialist.
