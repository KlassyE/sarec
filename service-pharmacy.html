Pharmacy

A fully-stocked on-site pharmacy — quality medicines dispensed by qualified pharmacists, right after your consultation.

On-Site Pharmacy — Convenient & Affordable

Having a fully stocked pharmacy right within our medical center means you don't have to make an extra trip to get your medications. After your consultation, simply walk to our pharmacy counter and have your prescription filled on the spot — quickly, affordably, and with the assurance that you're getting the right medicines.

What We Stock

Our pharmacy carries a comprehensive range of medications, including:

  • Antibiotics — for bacterial infections as prescribed by our doctors
  • Antimalarials — ACTs (Artemisinin-based Combination Therapies) and other treatments
  • Pain Relief — paracetamol, ibuprofen, diclofenac, and other analgesics
  • Chronic Disease Medications — metformin, glibenclamide, antihypertensives, inhalers for asthma
  • Antihistamines — for allergies and allergic reactions
  • Vitamins & Supplements — iron, folic acid, multivitamins, zinc
  • Gastrointestinal Medications — antacids, anti-diarrhoeal drugs, ORS
  • Topical Creams & Ointments — for skin conditions, wounds, and infections
  • Over-the-Counter (OTC) Products — commonly needed health products available without prescription

Quality & Safety

All medications stocked at our pharmacy are sourced from reputable, licensed pharmaceutical distributors. We maintain strict storage conditions, regularly check expiry dates, and ensure that every product meets the standards approved by Uganda's National Drug Authority (NDA).

Affordable Pricing

We understand that healthcare costs add up. That's why we keep our medication prices fair and competitive. We work to ensure that essential medications remain accessible and affordable for all our patients, whether insured or self-paying.

Pharmacist Guidance

Our pharmacy team is available to answer your questions about your medications — dosage instructions, potential side effects, drug interactions, and storage requirements. We want you to understand your treatment and take your medicines safely and correctly.
